# Real Estate Developer CRM / ERP Feature Landscape (India, with global benchmarks)

Research date: 24 Oct 2026. Most sources are vendor pages, vendor blogs (often competitor-written) and review aggregators (Capterra, Techjockey, Techimply, SoftwareSuggest, G2). Independent, hands-on comparisons for Indian developers were not found. Dated items are flagged inline. Codes used in the matrix in the last section: **N** = native per a cited source, **C** = custom build / partner / add-on / separate app, **?** = not found in sources (not proof that it is missing).

## Q1. Per-product modules and notable features

### Takeaway
The Indian developer market splits into (a) real-estate-native CRMs that run from lead to collections (Sell.Do, ANAROCK ACRM, DaeBuild, Makanify, PropFlo, HomeLead), (b) horizontal CRMs that developers configure (LeadSquared, Zoho, Salesforce, Kylas, Privyr), and (c) real estate / construction ERPs that include sales and CRM as one module among land, contracts, procurement and accounts (Farvision, StrategicERP, In4Suite, Xpedeon, SAP, Yardi). Sell.Do has the broadest documented real-estate CRM feature set (IRIS launch platform, broker app, demand letters, customer portal, AI voice agents, MCP server). Farvision and StrategicERP lead the ERP group.

### Inferences
- Developers' shortlists in 2026 typically contain Sell.Do, LeadSquared and Zoho (per a Sell.Do-authored piece), with Salesforce/Agentforce at the top end (Tata Realty, M3M) and Farvision / StrategicERP / SAP on the ERP side. The Sell.Do category definition (16 comparison rows) is a useful skeleton for a feature checklist, but it is written to favour Sell.Do.
- The main split is "CRM that stops at the deal" (LeadSquared, Zoho, Salesforce, Kylas, Privyr) versus "deal-to-collection" systems with unit inventory, cost sheets, demand letters and payouts (Sell.Do, ANAROCK ACRM, DaeBuild, Makanify, Farvision, StrategicERP). Horizontal CRMs reach parity only through custom builds or companion apps (Zoho Creator/Books/Sign; Salesforce SI projects).
- ERPs (Farvision, StrategicERP, In4Suite) differentiate on land/legal/RERA, contracts, procurement, project costing, accounting and facility/community management, which pure CRMs do not cover.

## Q2. Pre-sales features (lead capture, routing, scoring, site visits, channel partners, campaigns, messaging, telephony, AI)

### Takeaway
Multi-source lead capture, auto-assignment, WhatsApp/SMS/email automation, telephony with call recording, site-visit tracking and a mobile app are table stakes across almost every product. Leaders differentiate on CP portals with brokerage payouts, OTP/geo-verified site visits, AI voice agents in Indian languages, agentic follow-up, and launch-day EOI/queue systems.

## Q8. Normalized combined checklist: table stakes vs differentiators

### Takeaway
Across ~14 products, table stakes are lead capture/routing, communication automation, telephony, site-visit tracking, mobile apps, reports and APIs. Real-estate-specific table stakes for developer products are unit inventory, booking, payment plans, demand letters and collections. Leaders differentiate on CP lifecycle with payouts, launch-day allocation, AI voice/agentic automation, customer apps with construction updates, ERP-grade finance, and (currently unaddressed by CRMs) snag/possession/society handover.

- The category's own definition of a "full" developer CRM covers routing, field app, site visits, WhatsApp API, telephony, INR billing, AI scoring, call analysis, voice agents, CP payouts, unit inventory, cost sheets, demand letters, launch booking and deal-to-collection — [Sell.Do vs LeadSquared](https://www.sell.do/compare/sell-do-vs-leadsquared)

**Consolidated checklist (normalized), grouped by lifecycle**

A. Table stakes (expected by most Indian developers, 2026)
1. Lead capture from Meta/Google lead forms, website forms, portals (API or email parser), IVR/missed call, walk-in; source/campaign/sub-source tagging; de-duplication.
2. Rule-based auto-assignment (round-robin, by project, location, availability); reassignment on SLA breach; notifications.
3. Lead stages, follow-up tasks, reminders, notes, activity timeline.
4. WhatsApp Business API (templates, 2-way chat), SMS, email templates; basic drips.
5. Click-to-call with cloud telephony, call logging and recording.
6. Site-visit scheduling, reminders and outcome capture; mobile app for sales team.
7. Channel partner registration and lead submission (portal or app) with attribution.
8. Project/tower/unit inventory with statuses; price list; cost sheet/quote generation.
9. Booking form, payment plan selection, booking approval.
10. Payment schedule, milestone demand letters (bulk), receipts, ledger, overdue tracking.
11. Standard dashboards: source ROI, funnel, team activity, site visits, bookings, collections.
12. Role/hierarchy permissions, mobile apps, REST API, bulk import/export.

B. Differentiators (leaders)
1. Verified site visits (OTP, geo check-in) and CP vs direct attribution rules.
2. Full CP lifecycle: KYC, training/project library, live inventory view, slab/tier brokerage engine, invoice and payout dashboard, rewards, leaderboards, offline app.
3. Launch platform: EOIs, online tokens with receipts, queue/slot management, fair allocation, war room, zero double-booking.
4. AI: predictive scoring, AI voice agents in Indian languages, agentic follow-ups, call transcription/analysis/coaching, next-best-action, content generation, MCP/agent APIs.
5. Dynamic pricing/cost sheet with taxes and charges, multi-level discount approvals, parking/allied inventory.
6. KYC, allotment letter/agreement generation, e-sign, payment-gateway links.
7. Customer app/portal: payment schedule, receipts, documents, construction progress updates, tickets, referrals.
8. GST/e-invoicing, RERA document compliance, ERP/Tally/banking sync.
9. Possession and handover workflow; post-possession community/facility management; leasing.
10. ERP depth: land/legal/liaison, BOQ, contracts, procurement, project costing, consolidation.
11. Enterprise: ISO 27001, SSO, white-label apps, sandbox, offline apps, regional language UI.

C. Market gaps observed (no surveyed CRM documents natively)
1. Snag list capture with photo evidence and rectification tracking tied to the unit and RERA defect-liability period.
2. Society handover workflow (documents, corpus/maintenance fund transfer to RWA).
3. TDS (194-IA), home-loan disbursement tracking, interest-on-delay calculation (not documented in sources).
4. Audit trail, DPDP Act compliance, India data residency (not documented in sources).
