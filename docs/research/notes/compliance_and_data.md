# Compliance and Data Obligations for a Maharashtra Builder CRM (RERA, DPDP, TRAI, KYC/AML) — as of October 2026

Scope note: Maharashtra (MahaRERA) plus Indian national law. Research date 24 Oct 2026. Most MahaRERA order texts could not be fetched directly (maharera.maharashtra.gov.in, indiacode.nic.in and meity.gov.in PDFs returned 403 or were unreachable), so many MahaRERA details rest on CA-institute newsletters (WIRC-ICAI), law-firm notes and trade press. Each finding says what kind of source it comes from; check order numbers against the MahaRERA "Orders/Circulars" page before building hard-coded rules.

## 1. MahaRERA project registration and ongoing compliance (registration number, QPR, Form 1/2/3/4/5, extension, deregistration)

### Takeaway
A registered project must file a Quarterly Progress Report (QPR) on the MahaRERA portal within 20 days of each quarter end (Order 33/2022). The QPR covers units and garages booked, money received and spent, construction progress, plan changes and the status of the 70% account, including nil returns. Missing a QPR is now punished hard: in 2026 MahaRERA sent show-cause notices to 8,212 projects, threatening a ₹50,000 penalty, suspension or cancellation of registration, a ban on ads and sales, and frozen bank accounts. Form 5 (the CA's annual audit of the project accounts) is due within six months of the financial year end, i.e. by 30 September. Forms 1, 2 and 3 (architect, engineer and CA certificates) are needed for every withdrawal from the separate account.

### Cited Findings
- **Legal basis.** Developers must register projects and upload Forms 1, 2 and 3 every quarter on MahaRERA's website. The duty comes from Section 11 of the RERA Act and Rule 20 of the Maharashtra Rules. Order 18/2021 first set the QPR window at 7 days after quarter end. — [WIRC-ICAI MahaRERA update Sept 2021](https://old.wirc-icai.org/images/law-updates/Maharera-Sept-2021.pdf)
- **Current deadline and contents.** MahaRERA Order 33/2022 replaced the 7-day window with filing within 20 days of quarter end. QPR update categories include "Status of Booking of Plots/Apartments/Units" and "Present Status of Booking of Garages". Order 18/2021 had required booking status of units, garages and covered parking. — [WIRC-ICAI MahaRERA July 2022](https://old.wirc-icai.org/images/law-updates/maharera-july-2022.pdf) (secondary summary of the orders)
- **2026 enforcement round.** The January–March 2026 QPR was due on 20 April 2026. 8,212 projects missed it and got show-cause notices invoking Section 7 of the RERA Act, with 60 days to respond. Possible consequences are cancellation or suspension of registration, frozen bank accounts (including the escrow account), restrictions on advertising, marketing and sale, and a ₹50,000 penalty. QPRs must disclose units sold, funds received, money spent, changes to plans and construction progress. Developers must also report on the 70% escrow account even when it was not touched or there were no sales. Maharashtra has 33,029 registered projects. — [Outlook Money, 4 May 2026](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/maharera-flags-8212-projects-for-missing-qpr-deadline-gives-regulatory-warning); [Construction World](https://www.constructionworld.in/amp/latest-construction-news/real-estate-news/maharera-issues-notices-to-8212-projects-for-missing-qprs/91176)
- **Reinstatement.** Reinstating a cancelled registration requires a fresh registration process with a fine of ₹50,000 per project. One trade site describes a 10-day compliance window after a missed deadline, which conflicts with the 60 days given in 2026. — [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/maharera-flags-8212-projects-for-missing-qpr-deadline-gives-regulatory-warning); [The Realty Today](https://therealtytoday.com/news/regulatory/the-impact-of-neglecting-quarterly-progress-reports-in-real-estate-development/)
- **January 2025 suspensions.** On 9 January 2025 MahaRERA suspended the registration of 1,905 projects and froze their bank accounts. These developers had not answered show-cause notices issued in December 2024 about mandatory status disclosures for 10,771 projects. A further 3,499 lapsed projects were flagged for similar action. — [Angel One news](https://oga-prod.angelone.in/news/economy/maharera-tightens-homebuyer-protection-with-new-directives-in-2025)
- **Forms 1–4.** The architect's, engineer's and CA's certificates submitted to banks to release money from the separate account are Form 1, Form 2 and Form 3 respectively, under the MahaRERA (General) Regulations, 2017. Form 4 is the architect's completion certificate for each building or wing. — [ICAI/WIRC material via search; MahaRERA General Regulations 2017 on Casemine](https://www.casemine.com/act/in/5de8cab2eae8c7dd7fe2d274); [WIRC-ICAI RERA registration requirements](https://old.wirc-icai.org/images/material/RERA-registration-project-requirements.pdf)
- **Form 5.** Form 5 is the "Annual Report on Statement of Accounts" (Regulation 4), a CA's certificate on how project funds were used and withdrawn. The statutory basis is the proviso to Section 4(2)(l)(D): accounts must be audited by a practising CA within six months of the end of each financial year. — [Bajaj Finserv explainer](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/what-is-rera-form-5); [ICAI Navi Mumbai presentation, 24 May 2025](https://navimumbai.icai.org/resource/Image/24-05-2025_PPT_CA-Aditya-Zantye.pdf)
- **Form 5 due date in practice.** For FY 2020-21, Order 25/2021 dated 18 Oct 2021 extended the Form 5 deadline from 30 September 2021 to 31 December 2021. This confirms 30 September as the normal cutoff. — [WIRC-ICAI MahaRERA Dec 2021](https://old.wirc-icai.org/images/law-updates/maharera-dec-2021.pdf)
- **Extension (Section 7(3)).** MahaRERA uses Section 7(3) to extend projects rather than revoke them, often with conditions such as obtaining an Occupancy Certificate and filing monthly progress reports. Examples: a project about 80% complete was extended from 31.12.2023 to 30.06.2025, and another was extended to 31.12.2027. — [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/consumer-cases/maharashtra-rera-extention-adhiraj-construction-complete-project-253732); [LiveLaw](https://livelaw.in/consumer-cases/maharashtra-rera-extension-project-252971)
- **Allottee consent for further extensions.** A 2019 MahaRERA circular allows further extension only where the association of allottees resolves that the existing promoter may finish the project. Later orders refer to needing a 2/3 majority of allottees' consent. — [Mondaq](https://mondaq.co.uk/india/real-estate/1270734/extension-of-registration-of-a-project-without-consent-of-allottees-under-rera-in-maharashtra); [Unimont Coral order PDF via LiveLaw](https://livelaw.in/pdf_upload/unimont-coral-project-549090.pdf); [Argus Partners](https://www.argus-p.com/updates/updates/maharera-guidelines-on-extension-of-registration-of-a-real-estate-project/)
- **Deregistration.** Order 42/2023 created a route to deregister inactive projects. Prerequisites include zero allottees and no bookings, and no pending deregistration-related case before any forum. For lapsed projects, the Authority may revoke registration under Section 5 on complaint and has directed builders to apply for extension instead. — [King Stubb & Kasiva](https://ksandk.com/newsletter/maha-rera-de-registration-of-real-estate-projects/); [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/consumer-cases/maharera-builder-extension-construction-project-possession-homebuyers-261953)

### Inferences
- The CRM's booking register is the source data for the QPR booking table: unit, garage and parking allotment status, agreement status and amounts received. It needs a quarter-end snapshot and an export in a QPR-ready format, with alerts at quarter end + 10 days and + 20 days.
- Because nil returns are required (escrow status even with no sales or withdrawals), the CRM should generate a QPR task every quarter for every registered project and wing, regardless of activity.
- A "project compliance status" flag (active / lapsed / suspended / abeyance / ads banned / accounts frozen) should block new bookings, payment links and marketing sends for that project. MahaRERA's sanctions include bans on advertising, marketing and sale.
- A per-project register should track: registration number and validity, revised completion date, extension orders, Form 1/2/3 per withdrawal, Form 4 per wing, Form 5 per FY (due 30 Sept), and QPRs per quarter.

## 2. The 70% designated (separate) RERA bank account and how CRM receipts map to it

### Takeaway
Section 4(2)(l)(D) of the RERA Act requires 70% of amounts collected from allottees to be kept in a separate scheduled-bank account, used only for land and construction cost and withdrawn against Forms 1/2/3. For projects registered on or after 1 July 2024, MahaRERA Order 56/2024 (27 June 2024) requires three accounts in one scheduled bank:
- a Collection account, into which 100% of allottee money is paid
- the Separate account (70%)
- a Transaction account (30%)

Money moves out of the Collection account only by auto-sweep under a standing instruction.

### Cited Findings
- **The 70% rule.** Section 4(2)(l)(D) mandates that 70% of the funds collected from allottees be deposited in a separate account maintained in a scheduled bank. — [LiveLaw on the MahaRERA discussion paper](https://livelaw.in/consumer-cases/maharera-bank-account-real-estate-project-discussion-paper-252573)
- **Order 56/2024 and the three accounts.** MahaRERA Order No. 56/2024 dated 27 June 2024 applies to projects registered on or after 1 July 2024. The promoter must open all three accounts in a single scheduled bank before applying for registration: Collection (100%), Separate (70%) and Transaction (30%). No debits or withdrawals are allowed from the Collection account by cheque, card, internet banking, DD or bank guarantee. The only permitted route is auto-sweep to the Separate and Transaction accounts, at a 70:30 split set by a written standing instruction given when the accounts are opened. — [Circular summary (Scribd)](https://www.scribd.com/document/795860176/Circular-RERA-Update); [Fox Mandal](https://foxmandal.in/News/maharera-seeks-to-mandate-three-bank-accounts-for-projects/); [The Realty Today](https://www.therealtytoday.com/news/maharashtra-rera-proposes-mandatory-separate-bank-accounts-for-real-estate-developers)
- **Permitted uses of the 70% account.** Per the March 2024 discussion paper, money in the 70% account can pay land cost, construction cost and interest on loans, plus interest, compensation or refunds to allottees. — [LiveLaw](https://livelaw.in/consumer-cases/maharera-bank-account-real-estate-project-discussion-paper-252573)
- **Withdrawals.** Withdrawals need Forms 1, 2 and 3 (architect, engineer, CA). If no money is withdrawn in a quarter, the developer self-certifies the amount deposited. — [WIRC-ICAI / realtyhunting summary of QPR rules](https://realtyhunting.com/rera-quarterly-progress-report)
- **Account freezing for non-compliance.** Freezing bank accounts is an active enforcement tool: 1,905 projects in January 2025, and threatened again for QPR defaulters in 2026. — [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/market-updates/maharera-bold-step-freezing-over-1950-real-estate-project-accounts-to-safeguard-home-buyers); [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/maharera-flags-8212-projects-for-missing-qpr-deadline-gives-regulatory-warning)
- **Other states.** Tamil Nadu RERA adopted a similar three-account rule from 1 January 2026. This signals a trend, but it is not binding in Maharashtra. — [Angel One](https://oga-prod.angelone.in/news/economy/tamil-nadu-rera-implements-three-account-rule-for-real-estate-projects-from-january-1-2026)

### Inferences
- **Payment instructions.** For post-July-2024 projects, every demand letter, payment link and receipt should name only that project's Collection account. A payment received in any other account (e.g. a group company account) is a compliance exception the CRM should flag.
- **Receipt fields.** Each receipt should carry: project and wing, unit, allottee, receipt head (consideration, taxes, stamp duty or registration charges collected as pass-through, other charges), bank account credited, UTR/instrument, and date. Finance can then reconcile allottee receipts against the 70% sweep and the QPR or Form 5 figures.
- **Pass-through amounts.** Whether GST, stamp duty or society/maintenance deposits must flow through the Collection account is not settled by the sources found. The CRM should support a configurable "RERA-countable" flag per charge head.
- **Older projects.** Pre-July-2024 projects run on the older single 70% separate-account model, so the CRM should store an account-model attribute per project.

### Gaps
- I could not fetch the text of Order 56/2024 to confirm whether refunds to allottees on cancellation must be paid from the Separate or Transaction account, or exactly which charge heads count as "amounts realised from allottees".
- Maharashtra Rule 5 (withdrawal procedure) text was not retrieved.

## 3. Carpet area definition and sale-on-carpet-area disclosure; 10% advance cap

### Takeaway
Under RERA Section 2(k), carpet area is the net usable floor area of an apartment. It includes internal partition walls and excludes external walls, service shafts, exclusive balconies, verandahs and open terraces. Units must be priced and described on carpet area, and no more than 10% of the cost may be taken before a registered agreement for sale.

### Cited Findings
- **Definition.** Carpet area is the usable floor space inside an apartment. It counts internal partition walls but excludes external walls, service shafts, exclusive balconies, verandahs and open terraces. Common areas such as lifts and lobbies are excluded. — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/corporate-law/definition-carpet-area-rera-2016.html); [PSA Legal](https://www.psalegal.com/definition-of-carpet-area-its-interpretation/)
- **Pricing and disclosure.** RERA prohibits pricing on any measure other than carpet area. Promoters must state carpet area in promotional material and agreements. — [Piramal Realty explainer](https://www.piramalrealty.com/blog/post/what-does-rera-carpet-area-mean-and-how-is-it-calculated/); [LiveLaw on HRERA carpet-area regulations](https://livelaw.in/columns/hrera-real-estate-regulation-and-development-act-homebuyers-178239)
- **10% cap.** A builder cannot demand or accept more than 10% of the flat cost without entering into an agreement for sale (RERA Section 13). A 2026 commentary confirms the 10% pre-agreement cap and the 70% escrow rule still apply. — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/mumbai/consumer-connect-look-at-maharera-portal-for-homebuying-guidelines-says-expert); [PropNewz 2026](https://www.propnewz.com/blog/maharera-verification-mmr-buyer-2026)
- **Amenities disclosure.** Order 57/2024 (July 2024) requires detailed amenities information in Annexure-I of the sale agreement: the expected dates amenities become available, phased availability, and the date of handover to the residents' association. — [Mondaq](https://mondaq.com/india/real-estate/1501512/rera-update|-builders-now-requires-to-declare-all-the-amenities-alongwith-the-timeline)

### Inferences
- **Unit master.** The unit master should store RERA carpet area as the primary area in sq m (with sq ft shown), plus balcony, verandah and terrace areas as separate fields. Quotes, cost sheets, allotment letters and agreements should be generated from carpet area only. Super built-up or saleable area fields should be hidden or blocked from customer-facing documents.
- **10% hard stop.** The CRM should total all receipts per unit before the agreement is registered and block or alert once they exceed 10% of the agreement value.
- **Amenity dates.** Committed amenity dates from Annexure-I should become tracked milestones linked to customer communications.

### Gaps
- I could not retrieve the exact statutory wording of Section 2(k), Section 13, or the MahaRERA order on stating areas in sq m. The wording above is from secondary sources.

## 4. Advertisement and marketing rules; real estate agents and channel partners

### Takeaway
- **Every advertisement.** Every ad must show the MahaRERA registration number, the MahaRERA website address and a scannable QR code. The QR code has been required since 1 August 2023 under Order 46/2023, extended to agents by Order 46B/2023.
- **April 2025 circular.** From 8 April 2025 the QR code goes in the top right corner. The registration number and website must be in a font at least as large as the largest font used for contact details. Penalty is up to ₹50,000.
- **Agents.** Agents must be MahaRERA-registered and hold the MahaRERA Certificate of Competency (Order 41/2023). Promoters may deal only with registered, certified agents and must declare them on the portal.
- **PMLA.** Agents with turnover of ₹20 lakh or more are reporting entities under PMLA.

### Cited Findings
- **Section 11(2).** Section 11(2) obliges promoters to mention the Authority's website address and the project registration number prominently in advertisements and prospectuses. Several RERAs enforce this, criticising small fonts or vertical layouts. — [West Bengal RERA order via LiveLaw](https://livelaw.in/consumer-cases/west-bengal-rera-registration-website-addresses-advertisements-252995); [AP RERA advertisement guidelines](https://rera.ap.gov.in/RERA/Views/AdvertisementGuidelines.aspx); [Haryana RERA order](https://haryanarera.gov.in/assistancecontrol/viewOrderPdf/NzAxMzU=)
- **QR code mandate.** Order 46/2023 made QR codes compulsory in project ads from 1 August 2023, placed beside the registration number and website address and legible and scannable by software. Order 46B/2023 extended this to agents. Non-compliance is a violation punishable with a penalty of up to ₹50,000, minimum ₹10,000. — [WIRC-ICAI newsletter Sept 2023](https://old.wirc-icai.org/newsletters/2023/september/maharera.html); [TaxGuru on the QR order](https://taxguru.in/corporate-law/matter-non-display-quick-response-qr-code-promotions-advertisement-real-estate-projects-registered-maharera.html)
- **8 April 2025 circular.** The circular applies to developers and agents and covers all forms of advertisement: print, digital, hoardings, brochures and social media.
  - The QR code goes in the top right corner, clearly visible and scannable, and must link directly to the project's MahaRERA page.
  - The registration number and website link must be at least as large as the largest font used for contact details or addresses.
  - Light colours and visual clutter should be avoided.
  - Non-compliance must be fixed within 10 days of detection, after which action follows. The penalty is up to ₹50,000 under Sections 63 and 65.
  - Source: [The Realty Today, 12 Apr 2025](https://therealtytoday.com/news/regulatory/maharera-makes-qr-code-registration-number-mandatory-in-ads-50000-penalty-for-violations/); [Rentu](https://rentu.in/news/10000082/maharera-mandates-clear-display-of-registration-details-in-housing-ads)
- **Order 46C/2025.** A 2026 source cites "Order 46C/2025" as requiring every advertisement, brochure and website page to carry the registration number and QR code. — [PropNewz](https://www.propnewz.com/blog/maharera-verification-mmr-buyer-2026) (order number not independently verified)
- **Enforcement.** MahaRERA penalised 628 projects for missing QR codes or registration numbers in ads. — [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/amp/how-it-works/maharera-penalises-628-projects-over-absence-of-qr-codes-in-ads-registration-number-36609.htm)
- **Certificate of Competency.**
  - Order 41/2023 (10 Jan 2023) made the "MahaRERA Real Estate Agent Certificate of Competency" mandatory for agent registration and renewal from 1 May 2023. It covers individual agents, authorised signatories of firms, and employees who deal with homebuyers.
  - From 1 September 2023 promoters may list only certified agents.
  - Exams are run with IBPS.
  - MahaRERA suspended about 20,000 agents who lacked the certificate.
  - Source: [Housing.com](https://housing.com/news/maharera-insists-on-promoters-dealing-with-only-certified-agents); [The Realty Today](https://therealtytoday.com/news/regulatory/maharera-suspends-registration-of-over-20000-real-estate-agents-in-maharashtra/); [Housing.com](https://housing.com/news/maharera-cancels-registrations-of-20000-real-estate-agents/)
- **Declaring agents.** Promoters were required to declare agent details (name, address, projects handled) from 1 January 2024. Because this was poorly followed, a circular of 29 April 2024 directed projects to deal only with trained, MahaRERA-certified agents. Consequences can include rejection or revocation of registration and a monetary penalty. — [Housing.com (via search summary)](https://housing.com/news/maharera-insists-on-promoters-dealing-with-only-certified-agents)
- **Unregistered projects.** Section 10(a) bars agents from facilitating transactions in unregistered or expired projects. Agents must show their MahaRERA registration number on ads and promotional material. — [Open-exam-prep MahaRERA CoC study guide](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/in-maharera-agent-coc/agent-registration-compliance-conduct/agent-functions-duties-conduct) (secondary)
- **Agent certificate format.** Sample MahaRERA agent registration certificates (Form H) show numbers in the format A0XXXXXXXXXXX, five-year validity, and a condition not to facilitate sales in unregistered projects. — Source: sample MahaRERA Form H certificates posted on a large developer's channel-partner empanelment portal (cp.godrejproperties.com). The specific URLs are left out because each one identifies an individual agent.
- **PMLA for agents.** Notification G.S.R. 855(E) dated 29 Nov 2022 (replacing G.S.R. 798(E) of 28 Dec 2020) makes RERA-defined real estate agents with annual turnover of ₹20 lakh or more "persons carrying on designated business or profession", i.e. PMLA reporting entities. MahaRERA ordered such agents to disclose their principal officer and designated director. — [Ahlawat & Associates](https://www.ahlawatassociates.com/blog/mandatory-compliance-by-real-estate-agents-under-pmla); [Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/obligations-procedures-for-reporting-entities-under-pmla); [PropNewsTime](https://propnewstime.com/latestnewsstories/Mzgw/maharera-realtors-with-20lac-turnover-must-comply-with-pmla-act-2002)

### Inferences
- **Channel-partner master.** Store the MahaRERA agent registration number, validity dates, Certificate of Competency status for each signatory or employee, and PMLA status (turnover band). Block onboarding, lead attribution, brokerage booking and commission payout for unregistered, suspended, expired or uncertified partners. Using unregistered channel partners exposes both the agent (unregistered activity) and the promoter (project action), per MahaRERA's 2024 circular.
- **Marketing assets.** Every creative, landing page, brochure, WhatsApp template and social post should carry project registration number, MahaRERA URL and QR code, with a pre-publish compliance checklist (top-right QR, font-size rule) and an audit log of approved creatives. This record supports the 10-day rectification window.
- **Automatic lock.** Marketing for a project should be locked automatically when its registration is lapsed, suspended or in abeyance.

- I could not confirm whether agreements for sale must name the agent and agent registration number. No MahaRERA order was found.
- No MahaRERA rule was found on telecallers or in-house sales staff who are not "agents". Order 41/2023's coverage of "employees who interact with homebuyers" applies to agent firms, and whether promoters' own sales staff need certification was not confirmed.

## 5. Customer complaints, MahaRERA conciliation, compensation enforcement and record-keeping

### Takeaway
Buyers can file a formal complaint under Section 31 (fee ₹5,000) or use the MahaRERA Conciliation & Dispute Resolution Forum (about ₹1,000). Settlements reached in conciliation are binding. Since November 2025 an SOP requires compensation ordered by MahaRERA to be paid within 60 days. If it is not, MahaRERA decides a non-compliance application within about 4 weeks and may refer the developer to the civil court, where imprisonment of up to 3 months is possible.

### Cited Findings
- **Fees.** A formal Section 31 complaint carries a ₹5,000 filing fee and targets disposal within 60 days. Conciliation costs ₹1,000 (+GST) per a 2026 guide, or ₹1,000 per a 2020 article; one column says there is no fee. — [Open-exam-prep](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/in-maharera-agent-coc/maharera-rules-portal/maharera-conciliation-disputes); [S.S. Rana](https://ssrana.in/corporate-laws/rera-real-estate-laws-india/rera-complaint-cost-status-india/); [Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/maharera-bar-association-to-provide-free-legal-aid-to-needy-homebuyers-from-1st-february/59243.html); contradicted by [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/mumbai/consumer-connect-conciliation-is-preferable-over-litigation)
- **Conciliation procedure.** A conciliation request filed online is sent automatically to the other party. Once that party accepts within 7 days, the fee is paid and a conciliation bench is assigned. Pending complaints can be referred to conciliation only with both parties' consent. Settlement terms are binding and enforceable under Section 40. — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/corporate-law/procedure-referring-complaints-maharera-conciliation-dispute-resolution-forum-maharera-adjudicating-officer-maharera.html); [Punekar News](https://www.punekarnews.in/pune-mahareras-conciliation-forum-favourable-for-settling-home-buying-disputes/)
- **Hearing mode.** Since 11 August 2025, after a Bombay High Court direction, complainants may choose physical or online hearings. Complaints are still filed online. — [Angel One](https://oga-prod.angelone.in/news/economy/maharera-tightens-homebuyer-protection-with-new-directives-in-2025)
- **Compensation SOP (22 Nov 2025).** Developers must pay compensation within 60 days of a MahaRERA order. If unpaid, the buyer files a non-compliance application, which MahaRERA decides within 4 weeks (another report says recovery applications are heard within 30 days). Persistent defaulters are referred to the Principal Civil Court and may face imprisonment of up to 3 months. — [Angel One](https://oga-prod.angelone.in/news/economy/maharera-tightens-homebuyer-protection-with-new-directives-in-2025); [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/economy/maharera-stricter-sop-non-compliant-builders-risk-imprisonment-over-compensation-defaults); [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/mahareras-60-day-compensation-rule-what-homebuyers-need-to-know)
- **Recovery warrants.** On 22 Jan 2026 MahaRERA issued a recovery warrant in a Mumbai case, stressing that its orders are enforceable decrees. — [The Law Suits](https://thelawsuits.in/maharera-orders-recovery-warrant-after-8-year-rera-legal-battle/)
- **Interest rate.** The interest rate for delay or refund under Maharashtra Rule 18 is SBI's highest MCLR + 2% p.a., and it is used in MahaRERA and Bombay HC orders. — [Open-exam-prep](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/in-maharera-agent-coc/allottee-rights-duties-delays/delay-interest-compensation-math); [LegitQuest (Bombay HC)](https://www.legitquest.com/case/kedar-prabhakar-gorwadkar-and-ors-v-sachin-balkrishna-kulkarni-and-ors/20905B)
- **Parking.** In July 2025 MahaRERA directed a developer to allot parking in the same wing as the buyers' flats within 60 days, treating otherwise as a breach. — [Angel One](https://oga-prod.angelone.in/news/economy/maharera-tightens-homebuyer-protection-with-new-directives-in-2025)

### Inferences
- **Complaints register.** The CRM needs a complaints and disputes register linked to unit and allottee, with these fields:
  - channel (internal / MahaRERA s.31 / conciliation / Adjudicating Officer / appellate)
  - MahaRERA case number
  - hearing dates and mode
  - order date
  - amount ordered
  - 60-day payment deadline countdown
  - interest calculator (SBI highest MCLR + 2%, with a configurable rate table)
  - settlement terms
- **Record retention.** Allotment, payment, communication and possession records support both MahaRERA disputes (often years later) and PMLA, tax and DPDP duties. Retention should be policy-driven per document type, not "delete on purpose served".
- **Parking.** Parking allotment should be wing-aware, and the parking allotment number should be recorded in the allotment letter.

### Gaps
- No official MahaRERA fee schedule was retrieved, and the conciliation fee conflicts across sources.
- No MahaRERA rule was found that specifies how long a promoter must keep customer records. The Act's general book-keeping duties (s.4(2)(l)(D) audit; agents' records under s.10) were not quantified.
- The Rule 19 refund timeline (reportedly 30 days) appears only in a study guide and is unverified.

## 6. Booking, allotment letter and cancellation (Maharashtra-specific); SROs

### Takeaway
MahaRERA's model allotment letter (July 2022) and model agreement for sale are mandatory. Promoters may change the cancellation terms only in the buyer's favour. The deduction on cancellation by the allottee is capped at 2%, tiered by how many days after allotment the buyer cancels (full refund within 15 days; up to 1% for 16–30 days). Order 38/2022 makes certain clauses of the model agreement non-negotiable, and conflicting clauses are void. Developer SROs (Order 10/2019) act as the recognised representatives for project registration.

### Cited Findings
- **Model allotment letter.** Promoters must use the model allotment letter approved in July 2022 when applying for registration. They may change the cancellation terms only by increasing the number of days within which the allottee can cancel and decreasing the deduction percentage. Required items include "refund and deduction on cancellation by allottee". — [AZB Partners, 30 Jun 2022](https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/maharera-model-allotment-letter-and-agreement-for-sale/); [Argus Partners](https://www.argus-p.com/updates/updates/maharera-proforma-of-allotment-letter-and-agreement-for-sale/); [WIRC-ICAI Sept 2022](https://old.wirc-icai.org/images/law-updates/maharera-sept-2022.pdf)
- **Cancellation deduction.** The deduction on cancellation by the allottee is capped at 2%. A press explainer gives the tiers: full refund within 15 days of the allotment letter, up to 1% deduction for 16–30 days; later tiers up to the 2% cap were not confirmed. Sources cite different order numbers (31/2022, 35/2022). — [Argus Partners](https://www.argus-p.com/updates/updates/maharera-proforma-of-allotment-letter-and-agreement-for-sale/); [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/mumbai/consumer-connect-look-at-maharera-portal-for-homebuying-guidelines-says-expert)
- **Non-negotiable clauses.** Order 38/2022 (13 Dec 2022) makes certain clauses of the model agreement (Annexure 'A' to Rule 10(1)) non-negotiable. Conflicting clauses in a registered agreement are void. Delay interest at SBI highest MCLR + 2% is one example. — [WIRC-ICAI Jan 2023](https://old.wirc-icai.org/images/law-updates/maharera-jan-23.pdf); [WIRC-ICAI Dec 2023](https://wirc-icai.org/html/Newsletter/member/December-2023/maharera.html)
- **Forfeiture before allotment.** The MahaRERA Appellate Tribunal held a reservation-form clause forfeiting 10% to be "ex-facie unreasonable, unfair and inequitable" where no allotment letter or agreement existed. It held that the right to reserve includes the right to withdraw. — [Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/right-to-reserve-flat-includes-right-to-withdraw-cannot-ask-allottee-to-forfeit-payment-rules-maharera-tribunal/63705.html); [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/news-updates/right-to-reserve-flat-includes-right-to-withdraw-cant-ask-allottee-to-forfeit-payment-maharashtra-rera-appellate-tribunal-173299)
- **Developer SROs.**
  - Order 10/2019 (11 Oct 2019) created the framework for SROs of promoters, with a code of conduct.
  - Only SRO representatives are recognised as developers' representatives for registering new projects.
  - Seven SROs were recognised as of January 2025, all developer associations.
  - In January 2025 MahaRERA lowered the threshold for non-MMR SROs to 200 projects (from 500; the sources disagree on whether the basis is members or projects) and capped SRO representatives' tenure at 2 years.
  - SRO registration is reportedly valid for 5 years.
  - Source: [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/mumbai/maharera-lowers-sro-threshold-to-200-projects-for-non-mmr-areas); [Punekar News](https://www.punekarnews.in/?p=194598); [WIRC-ICAI Sept 2023](https://old.wirc-icai.org/images/law-updates/maharera-sept-2023.pdf); [WIRC-ICAI Circular 42 on SRO role](https://old.wirc-icai.org/images/material/Circular-No42-Role-of-SRO-AshwinShah.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Templates and cancellation.** Allotment letters and agreements should be generated only from locked MahaRERA model templates. The editable cancellation fields should be validated so days can only go up and deduction percentages only down (cap 2%). Refund amounts should be calculated automatically from the allotment-letter date and cancellation-request date.
- **Pre-allotment refunds.** Bookings at "EOI" or "reservation" stage, before the allotment letter, should default to full refund, given the tribunal's view on forfeiture.
- **SRO field.** Store the developer's SRO membership and representative per project, since project registration filings go through SRO representatives.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm the full tier schedule beyond 30 days (e.g. 31–60 days and beyond 60 days) or the definitive order number from MahaRERA's own text.
- No MahaRERA-recognised SRO for real estate agents (as opposed to developers) was found. I found no reliable source that agents must join an SRO.

## 7. DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025 — status as of October 2026 and obligations relevant to a builder CRM

### Takeaway
The DPDP Rules were notified on 13/14 November 2025 and phase in as follows:
- **Immediately:** Data Protection Board provisions.
- **13 November 2026:** consent-manager provisions.
- **13 May 2027:** the core data-fiduciary obligations (notice, consent, security, breach notification, rights, retention).

In January 2026 MeitY proposed shortening the 18 months to 12, which would make 13 November 2026 the full-compliance date. As of this research I found no confirmation that the change was notified. It must be checked now, because that date is about three weeks away.

Penalties go up to ₹250 crore for security failures, ₹200 crore for breach-notification failures or child-data violations, and ₹50 crore for other breaches.

### Cited Findings
- **Notification.** Parliament enacted the DPDP Act on 11 Aug 2023. The government notified the DPDP Rules, 2025 on 14 November 2025 after 6,915 consultation inputs. — [PIB backgrounder, 17 Nov 2025](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf). AZB Partners dates the notification 13 Nov 2025. — [AZB Partners](https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/update-indias-digital-personal-data-protection-framework-comes-into-effect/). One source gives the Gazette number as G.S.R. 846(E) (unverified). — [search summary of Hogan Lovells / KS&K](https://ksandk.com/data-protection-and-data-privacy/dpdp-act-rules-2026-compliance-deadline/)
- **Phases.**
  - Phase I, from 13 Nov 2025: Rules 1, 2 and 17–21 (Board, definitions).
  - Phase II, from 13 Nov 2026: Rule 4 (consent-manager registration).
  - Phase III, from 13 May 2027: Rules 3, 5–16, 22 and 23 (notice, consent, fiduciary obligations, children, rights, cross-border, Board inquiry and penalty powers).
  - Source: [AZB Partners](https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/update-indias-digital-personal-data-protection-framework-comes-into-effect/); [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/breaking-govt-notifies-final-dpdp-rules-sets-staggered-rollout-with-key-obligations-for-data-fiduciaries-84201.htm); [India Briefing](https://india-briefing.com/news/india-dpdp-compliance-timeline-enforcement-2026-27-44740.html)
- **Proposed acceleration (pending).** In January 2026 MeitY sought views (comments due 4 Feb 2026) on cutting fiduciary obligations from 18 to 12 months, which would move full compliance to 13 Nov 2026. It also proposed making Rules 15 and 23 effective immediately and Rule 8(3) within three months. No source found confirms a final amendment. — [S.S. Rana](https://ssrana.in/articles/meity-plans-to-cut-short-dpdp-compliance-timeline-and-notify-cross-border-restrictions-for-sdfs/); [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/meity-seeks-industry-views-on-fast-tracking-dpdp-act-rollout-proposes-12-month-compliance-timeline-88332.htm); [Mondaq](https://webiis08.mondaq.com/india/privacy-protection/1760380/compression-of-dpdp-enforcement-timeline-proposed-by-meity)
- **Notice and consent (Rule 3).** Every Data Fiduciary must give a separate consent notice that is clear, understandable and distinct from other information. It must include an itemised description of the personal data and the specified purposes. It must also link to the website or app where the person can withdraw consent, exercise their rights and complain to the Board. Consent must be clear and informed and can be withdrawn at any time. — [PIB](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf); [AZB Partners](https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/update-indias-digital-personal-data-protection-framework-comes-into-effect/)
- **Purpose limitation and principles.** The Act's principles are consent and transparency, purpose limitation, data minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation, security safeguards and accountability. — [PIB](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf)
- **Security safeguards (Rule 6).** Minimum measures are encryption, masking or tokenisation; access controls; logging and monitoring; backups and continuity; and keeping logs and related data for 1 year. Security obligations must be written into contracts with data processors. — [AZB Partners](https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/update-indias-digital-personal-data-protection-framework-comes-into-effect/)
- **Breach notification (Rule 7).**
  - Without delay: a preliminary report to the Board (nature, extent, timing, location, likely impact).
  - Without delay: notice to every affected person, in plain language, covering what happened, likely consequences, protective steps and a contact.
  - Within 72 hours: a detailed report to the Board (extendable on written request) covering mitigation, findings, remediation and the notices given.
  - CERT-In reporting within 6 hours applies separately.
  - Source: [AZB Partners](https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/update-indias-digital-personal-data-protection-framework-comes-into-effect/); [PIB](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf)
- **Retention and erasure (Rule 8).** All fiduciaries must keep personal data, traffic data and processing logs for at least 1 year for specified government purposes. After that, data is erased unless law requires longer retention. Fixed 3-year erasure periods apply only to large e-commerce (20 million+ users), online gaming (5 million+) and social media (20 million+) platforms, with 48 hours' notice before erasure. Real estate developers are not in these classes. — [AZB Partners](https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/update-indias-digital-personal-data-protection-framework-comes-into-effect/)
- **Contact point.** Every fiduciary must publish contact details for personal-data queries, either a designated officer or a DPO. — [PIB](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf)
- **Rights.** Data principals can access, correct, update and erase their data, nominate another person to act for them, and seek grievance redress. Fiduciaries must respond within a maximum of 90 days and must publish how to exercise these rights, including identity verification. — [PIB](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf); [AZB Partners](https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/update-indias-digital-personal-data-protection-framework-comes-into-effect/)
- **Children and persons with disabilities.** A child's data needs verifiable parental or guardian consent. For a person with a disability, consent comes from a verified lawful guardian. — [PIB](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf)
- **Significant Data Fiduciaries.** SDFs have additional duties: annual DPIA, audits, an India-based DPO and possible localisation. — [AZB Partners](https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/update-indias-digital-personal-data-protection-framework-comes-into-effect/)
- **Consent managers.** Consent managers must be Indian companies and keep consent records for 7 years. — [AZB Partners](https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/update-indias-digital-personal-data-protection-framework-comes-into-effect/)
- **Penalties.** Up to ₹250 crore for failing to keep reasonable security safeguards; up to ₹200 crore each for not notifying the Board or people of a breach and for child-data violations; up to ₹50 crore for other violations. — [PIB](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf)

#### Aadhaar storage
- **Voluntary collection only.** The 2018 Puttaswamy (Aadhaar) judgment lets private entities collect Aadhaar numbers only voluntarily, with explicit, informed consent. — [Khaitan & Co ERGO, 10 Dec 2025](https://www.khaitanco.com/sites/default/files/2025-12/ERGO%20-%20Aadhaar%20Data%20Vault%20-%2010%20December%202025.pdf)
- **Aadhaar Data Vault scope.**
  - UIDAI Circular No. 8 of 2025 (18 Jul 2025) and the FAQs of 3 Nov 2025 limit ADV requirements to requesting entities (AUAs/KUAs/Sub-AUAs/Sub-KUAs). They do not apply to offline-verification entities (OVSEs) or to entities collecting Aadhaar for internal purposes without UIDAI verification.
  - Where ADV applies: Aadhaar number, UID token, e-KYC XML and Aadhaar PDF go in the vault, accessed by reference keys, with HSM and AES-256 encryption.
  - Hosting is allowed only on-premises, on a MeitY-empanelled Government Community Cloud, or via ADV-as-a-service.
  - Demographic data may sit outside the vault only if it is not mapped to the Aadhaar number.
  - Source: [Khaitan & Co ERGO](https://www.khaitanco.com/sites/default/files/2025-12/ERGO%20-%20Aadhaar%20Data%20Vault%20-%2010%20December%202025.pdf); [UIDAI ADV FAQ 2017](https://uidai.gov.in/images/resource/FAQs_Aadhaar_Data_Vault_v1_0_13122017.pdf)
- **Masking.** Vendor and blog sources say businesses that collect Aadhaar copies should mask the first 8 digits (and the QR code) before storing them. No primary UIDAI text was found for private-entity copy masking. — [Cashfree docs](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/secure-id/digilocker/aadhaar-masking.md); [Suvit](https://suvit.io/post/aadhaar-card-masking)
- **Circular 14 of 2025 (unconfirmed).** A blog reports a UIDAI Circular No. 14 of 2025 (4 Nov 2025) that supersedes earlier ADV circulars and bars hashed Aadhaar numbers as reference keys. This was not confirmed against UIDAI. — [Finahub blog](https://blog.finahub.com/2026/08/aadhaar-data-vault-compliance-uidai-circular-14.html?m=1)

### Inferences
- **Who is the fiduciary.** A builder is a Data Fiduciary for leads, allottees, co-applicants, nominees and channel-partner staff. Channel partners and CRM, SMS or WhatsApp vendors are processors (or independent fiduciaries) and need DPDP-compliant contracts.
- **Consent register.** Record notice version, purposes (e.g. "sales follow-up", "marketing of other projects", "loan facilitation"), channel, timestamp, source (site visit form, portal lead, ad lead) and withdrawal. Lead imports from portals and channel partners should carry provenance and consent evidence.
- **Data-rights tracker.** A 90-day SLA, identity verification step and nominee handling.
- **Breach register.** Timers for 6 hours (CERT-In), "without delay" plus 72 hours (Board), and customer notification templates.
- **Retention schedule by record type.** Statutory retention (RERA disputes, PMLA 5 years for reporting entities, tax records) overrides erasure. Dead leads with no legal basis should be purged or anonymised. Processing logs must be kept for at least 1 year.
- **Aadhaar and PAN.** Store masked Aadhaar only (last 4 digits) and keep any Aadhaar copy encrypted with restricted access. Prefer DigiLocker or offline-XML flows over full numbers. Encrypt PAN at field level with masked display and role-based access.
- **Timeline risk.** Phase III is only months away (May 2027), and possibly 13 Nov 2026 if the 12-month proposal was adopted. The CRM should be DPDP-ready now.

### Gaps
- I could not fetch the Gazette/MeitY text of the Rules (403). Rule numbers and the 72-hour detail come from law-firm summaries.
- Final status of the January 2026 timeline-compression proposal is unknown. It must be checked on MeitY or the e-Gazette before relying on May 2027.
- No primary source was found for specific PAN storage rules for private entities, beyond general DPDP security.
- The primary UIDAI rule on masking physical Aadhaar copies collected by private businesses was not found.
- Whether a real estate developer can be designated an SDF is unknown; it is unlikely for most.

## 8. Telemarketing and messaging rules (TRAI TCCCPR 2018 + 2025 amendment, DLT, NCPR/DND, calling windows, WhatsApp, email)

### Takeaway
All commercial SMS must go through TRAI's DLT system: entity (PE ID), header, content template, and a PE–telemarketer chain. Promotional messages cannot reach DND/NCPR subscribers without registered digital consent and are limited to daytime windows (sources say 9 AM–9 PM or 10 AM–9 PM). The February 2025 TCCCPR amendment brought in:
- the 140 series for promotional voice calls and 1600 for service/transactional calls, with no 10-digit numbers for telemarketing
- a 90-day bar on re-asking consent after an opt-out
- action at 5 complaints in 10 days
- 15-day barring and then 1-year blacklisting of offending senders

WhatsApp requires contextual opt-in and approved templates outside the 24-hour service window. DoCA's guidelines on unsolicited business communication were still in draft as of mid-2026.

### Cited Findings
- **Numbering (Feb 2025 amendment).** Promotional calls must use the 140 series, and 10-digit numbers are barred for telemarketing. Service and transactional calls use a separate series: 1600 per one source, "160" per another. — [Fonearena](https://www.fonearena.com/blog/446114/trai-tcccpr-2025-tougher-rules-spam-calls-sms.html); [Bar & Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/view-point/trais-crackdown-on-spam-calls-and-ai-driven-telemarketing); [JSA Prism Feb 2025](https://www.jsalaw.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/JSA-Prism-Telecommunications_February-2025_TCCCPR-amendments.Final_.pdf)
- **Consent (Feb 2025 amendment).** Senders cannot ask again for consent for 90 days after a customer opts out. Consent tied to an ongoing transaction lasts only 7 days. Inferred consent for service or transactional communication lasts only for the contract period. — [Fonearena](https://www.fonearena.com/blog/446114/trai-tcccpr-2025-tougher-rules-spam-calls-sms.html)
- **Sender penalties.** First violation: all the sender's telecom resources are barred from outgoing services for 15 days. Later violations: disconnection for 1 year and blacklisting across all access providers. The complaint threshold for action against unregistered senders fell from 10 complaints in 7 days to 5 in 10 days. Access providers that misreport face ₹2 lakh, ₹5 lakh and ₹10 lakh fines for successive offences. — [Fonearena](https://www.fonearena.com/blog/446114/trai-tcccpr-2025-tougher-rules-spam-calls-sms.html); [Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/spam-calls-sms-regulations-tightened-by-trai-5-complaints-to-trigger-action-against-spammer/76368.html)
- **DLT registration layers.**
  - Entity: Principal Entity registration on a DLT portal with PAN/GST, about ₹5,900 + GST with annual renewal.
  - Header: 6 alphanumeric characters for transactional/service messages, 6 digits for promotional.
  - Content templates: fixed text with declared typed variables, approved in 24–72 hours, with typed variables required for templates registered after the October 2024 update.
  - PE–TM chain: required since December 2024, without which messages are blocked.
  - Source: [Telerivet guide](https://www.telerivet.com/blog/india-sms-compliance-trai-dlt-registration-and-tcccpr-guide); [MSG91 scrubbing rules](https://msg91.com/help/dlt-template-scrubbing-filtering-rules)
- **Header suffixes.** From 6 May 2025, after the TCCCPR 2025 amendment, operators add a category suffix to headers: -T (transactional), -S (service), -P (promotional), -G (government). The "Service Explicit" category was discontinued on 7 May 2025 and its templates moved to Promotional. — [Telerivet](https://www.telerivet.com/blog/india-sms-compliance-trai-dlt-registration-and-tcccpr-guide)
- **DND reach.** Transactional and service messages can reach DND numbers at any time. Promotional messages cannot reach DND numbers. — [Telerivet](https://www.telerivet.com/blog/india-sms-compliance-trai-dlt-registration-and-tcccpr-guide)
- **Time window (sources conflict).** Telerivet says promotional SMS may be sent only between 9 AM and 9 PM IST and are dropped outside it. Other CPaaS documentation in the same search states 10:00 AM–9:00 PM, possibly extendable with explicit digital consent. — [Telerivet](https://www.telerivet.com/blog/india-sms-compliance-trai-dlt-registration-and-tcccpr-guide); contradicted by [Sent.dm](https://www.sent.dm/resources/india-sms-pricing) / [Netcore DLT guidelines](https://emaildocs.netcorecloud.com/docs/dlt-guidelines) (search-summary attribution; check the TCCCPR text)
- **Header and template misuse.** TRAI has directed operators to bar unregistered telemarketers and to re-verify and block unverified headers and templates. — [Tribune India](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/nation/trai-cracks-down-on-unauthorised-telemarketers-directs-telcos-to-stop-misuse-of-headers-message-templates-480421); [Vikaspedia](https://www.vikaspedia.in/news/trai-issues-directions-to-access-providers-to-stop-misuse-of-message-templates-and-to-curb-unauthorized-promotions-using-telecom-resources)
- **DoCA guidelines (draft only).**
  - Draft guidelines on unsolicited and unwarranted business communication were issued on 20 Jun 2024. Under the draft, communications without consent, ignoring DND, or without caller identification count as "unsolicited".
  - A further draft, "Pesky, Promotional and Unsolicited Commercial Communications" Guidelines 2026, was open for comment in July 2026.
  - No final notification was found.
  - Source: [Lexplosion](https://lexplosion.in/department-of-consumer-affairs-invites-comments-on-draft-guidelines-for-prevention-of-unsolicited-and-unwarranted-business-communication-2024-by-05-08-2024/); [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2024/06/223-department-of-consumer-affars-guidelines-spam-calls-messages/); [FIDC comments, Jul 2026](https://www.fidcindia.org.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Comments-on-the-Draft-Guidelines-for-Prevention-and-Regulation-of-Pesky-Promotional-and-Unsolicited-Commercial-Communications-2026.pdf)
- **AI telemarketing.** TRAI is reported to be considering further rules on AI-based telemarketing and consent verification. — [S.S. Rana](https://ssrana.in/articles/trais-crackdown-on-spam-calls-and-ai-driven-telemarketing/)
- **WhatsApp Business (secondary vendor sources).**
  - Inside the 24-hour window after a customer message, businesses can reply free-form.
  - Outside it, only pre-approved, paid templates may be sent.
  - Templates are classed as marketing, utility or authentication, and unclear ones are treated as marketing.
  - Marketing templates need recipient opt-in, collected in-line and in context.
  - WhatsApp may limit delivery of marketing templates based on engagement.
  - Source: [Courier guide (updated Sept 2026)](https://www.courier.com/guides/whatsapp-message-types); [SMSmode](https://www.smsmode.com/en/whatsapp-business-api-customer-care-window-ou-templates-comment-les-utiliser/); [Amio policy summary](https://docs.amio.io/docs/whatsapp-policy); [MoEngage](https://www.moengage.com/blog/whatsapps-latest-deliverability-rules/)

### Inferences
- **Telecalling module.** Scrub every number against NCPR/DND preference and the CRM's own opt-out list before a promotional call or SMS. Run promotional campaigns only from 140-series numbers through a registered telemarketer. Enforce the time window conservatively (10:00–21:00 IST satisfies both readings). Block re-requesting consent for 90 days after an opt-out. Expire "transaction" consent after 7 days. Log every call and SMS with header, template ID and consent reference.
- **Message categories.** Payment reminders, demand letters, possession notices and OTPs should be registered as Service or Transactional templates. Launch offers and new-project promotions are Promotional (-P), DND-restricted and time-windowed.
- **Lead consent.** One consent record should map to both the DPDP purpose and the TRAI category, with source evidence. For leads from portals and channel partners, the CRM should require the partner's consent evidence before any outbound promotional contact.
- **WhatsApp.** Keep an opt-in register (timestamp, wording, channel) and keep templates in sync with the BSP. Distinguish utility templates (payment due, site-visit confirmation) from marketing.
- **Email.** I found no India-specific commercial email statute in this research. Treat email marketing as governed by DPDP consent and purpose limitation, with an unsubscribe link as good practice.

## 9. KYC/AML and cash rules (PMLA, sections 269SS/269ST, SFT/Form 61A; Income-tax Act 2025 transition)

### Takeaway
- **PMLA.** Developers are not named reporting entities under PMLA. Real estate agents with turnover of ₹20 lakh or more are, under G.S.R. 855(E) of 29 Nov 2022, with KYC, CTR/STR filing and 5-year records.
- **Advances (s.269SS).** No advance or other sum of ₹20,000 or more in connection with transfer of immovable property may be accepted except by account-payee cheque, draft or bank transfer. Penalty under s.271D equals the amount.
- **Cash (s.269ST).** No cash receipt of ₹2 lakh or more from one person in a day, for one transaction or for one event. Penalty under s.271DA equals the amount.
- **SFT.** Cash receipts above ₹2 lakh for goods or services by tax-audited persons are reported in the SFT (Form 61A) by 31 May, with a penalty of ₹500 a day.
- **Transition.** The Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the 1961 Act from 1 April 2026. New section numbers (e.g. s.221 for the cash limit) are reported but not verified.

### Cited Findings
- **PMLA for agents.** Notification G.S.R. 855(E) (29 Nov 2022), replacing G.S.R. 798(E) (28 Dec 2020), designates RERA-defined real estate agents with annual turnover of ₹20 lakh or more as "persons carrying on designated business or profession", i.e. reporting entities. — [Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/obligations-procedures-for-reporting-entities-under-pmla); [Ahlawat & Associates](https://www.ahlawatassociates.com/blog/mandatory-compliance-by-real-estate-agents-under-pmla); [VJM Global](https://www.vjmglobal.com/blog/jewellers-real-estate-agents-under-purview-pmla)
- **Agent PMLA duties.** Covered agents must register on FIU-IND FINnet, appoint a principal officer (and designated director), do client KYC with beneficial-owner identification, file cash and suspicious transaction reports, and keep records for 5 years. MahaRERA ordered agents above ₹20 lakh turnover to disclose their principal officer and designated director. — [CalcGuru (secondary)](https://calcguru.in/?p=4955); [PropNewsTime](https://propnewstime.com/latestnewsstories/Mzgw/maharera-realtors-with-20lac-turnover-must-comply-with-pmla-act-2002)
- **Section 269SS.** As amended, it bars accepting any sum, as advance or otherwise, in relation to transfer of immovable property of ₹20,000 or more other than by account-payee cheque or draft or electronic clearing through a bank account. The penalty is under s.271D. — [ITAT order (Income Tax Appellate Tribunal)](https://itat.gov.in/public/files/upload/1748429292-w9NDEn-1-TO.pdf); [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/section-269ss-269t)
- **Section 269ST.** It bars receiving ₹2 lakh or more in cash from one person in a day, in one transaction, or in transactions relating to one event or occasion. The penalty under s.271DA equals the amount, unless the person shows good and sufficient reasons (1961 Act). — [Income Tax Department](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/mode-of-receipts-and-payments); [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/?p=1030489)
- **Income-tax Act, 2025 (unverified mapping).** The 2025 Act replaced the 1961 Act from 1 April 2026. One compliance blog maps the cash-receipt limit to new Section 221 (₹2 lakh unchanged) and other cash limits to s.34(3) and ss.219/220. It claims penalties are now mandatory at 100% with no reasonable-cause defence. The draft Bill had proposed ₹3 lakh, but ₹2 lakh was kept on enactment. — [IncorpX blog](https://www.incorpx.io/blog/cash-transaction-limits-small-business-2026); [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/?p=1030489) (single secondary source; the "no reasonable cause" claim conflicts with the 1961 position)
- **SFT under Rule 114E.**
  - Sale or purchase of immovable property for ₹30 lakh or more (or stamp value ₹30 lakh or more) is reported by the Registrar or Sub-Registrar, not the developer.
  - Cash receipts above ₹2 lakh for sale of goods or services by any person liable to tax audit under s.44AB are reported in the SFT.
  - The due date is 31 May following the FY.
  - Late filing attracts ₹500 a day under s.271FA.
  - Source: [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/form-61a); [BankBazaar on Rule 114E](https://bankbazaar.com/pan-card/rule-114e.html); [Motilal Oswal](https://www.motilaloswal.com/personal-finance/tax/form-61a-purpose-transactions-reported-components-penalty)

### Inferences
- **Cash limits in the CRM.** Receipts should hard-block cash above ₹19,999 in aggregate per unit and allottee for any advance or consideration (s.269SS, which is stricter than 269ST for property). Cash repayments or refunds should be blocked as well (s.269T is the mirror provision; not verified this session). Cash flags should feed the SFT data pull.
- **Channel-partner records.** Brokerage-paid partners above ₹20 lakh turnover are PMLA reporting entities. The CRM should store their FIU registration and principal officer details, and partners may ask the developer for buyer KYC, beneficial ownership and source of funds.
- **Buyer KYC.** Even though developers are not PMLA reporting entities, collecting PAN (or Form 60), proof of address and source-of-funds evidence (home loan sanction, bank transfer UTRs) supports s.269SS/ST compliance, registration and stamp duty. Store these as KYC fields with masking and encryption under DPDP.
- **Section references.** The CRM should keep statutory references configurable, given the 1 April 2026 renumbering.
